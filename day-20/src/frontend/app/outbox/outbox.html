Day 20 · Transactional outbox

Outbox

Creating a quote writes the quote row and an outbox row in one EF transaction. A separate relay polls unsent rows, publishes each to the quote-events topic, and marks SentAt only after the publish succeeds — at-least-once delivery, deduplicated by MessageId on the Day 19 consumer.
