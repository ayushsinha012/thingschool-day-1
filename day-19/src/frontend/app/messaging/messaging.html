Day 19 · Service Bus topics + DLQ

Messaging

POST /api/messaging/publish sends one message to the quote-events topic. Two subscriptions (sub-audit, sub-notifications) each get their own independent copy — fan-out, not competing consumers. Within each subscription, two concurrent worker slots compete for messages and every handler dedupes on MessageId before doing any work.
