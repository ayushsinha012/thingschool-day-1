Lesson learned from the dev deployment (see dev-deployment.txt): the
Container App's system-assigned identity does not exist until the
Container App resource itself is created, but the first revision cannot
provision until that identity already holds AcrPull / Key Vault Secrets
User / Service Bus roles - a circular bootstrap dependency that caused
dev's first attempt to time out ("Operation expired"). For prod, that
was avoided proactively: the deployment was started, then this session
polled `az containerapp show --query identity.principalId` every 15s
until the Container App resource (and its real principalId) existed,
and immediately granted the same roles infra/modules/acrpull.bicep and
infra/modules/identity.bicep declare, before ARM's revision-provisioning
wait could time out.
